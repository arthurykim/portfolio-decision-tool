    Most backtesters quietly flatter themselves.

    Four places where this one refuses to.

    Real T-bill rate

    A 0% risk-free rate inflates every Sharpe ratio. We derive the actual rate from BIL over the same window.

    Inflation-adjusted

    7% nominal in a 5% inflation year is under 2% real. CAGR is restated from BLS CPI-U data.

    Survivorship bias, measured

    406 dated index changes replayed back to 1976. We publish the bias as a number instead of disclaiming it.

    Assumptions stated

    Daily rebalancing, no fees, no taxes. That flatters results — and we say so rather than burying it.
