About

What the numbers mean

CAGR

Compound annual growth rate — the steady yearly return that gets from the start value to the end value. Lets you compare periods of different lengths.

Total return

The overall percentage change over the whole period, not annualized. +100% means the money doubled.

Volatility

How much daily returns swing, annualized. Stocks run ~15–20%, bonds ~4–6%. Higher volatility means a bumpier ride.

Sharpe ratio

Return per unit of risk (CAGR ÷ volatility here). Above ~0.5 is decent for a passive portfolio; above 1.0 over long periods is very good.

Max drawdown

The worst peak-to-trough loss in the period — what you'd have lost buying at the worst top. Deep drawdowns are where investors give up.

1D · 1W · 1M · YTD · 1Y · 5Y · All

Price change over the trailing day, week, month, year-to-date, one year, five years, or the fund's full history.
